What your card shows

Privacy

Advanced

Quick hide from anywhere: press Alt+Shift+M in your browser (change it on the browser's extension shortcuts page), click the extension's toolbar icon, or run ytm-beacon hide / ytm-beacon show in a terminal.

Troubleshooting

If something isn't working, these help you (or us) find out why.


  


  

    Update or uninstall
    
Run these in a terminal (on Windows, PowerShell):

    
    
      
  • Update to the latest version: ytm-beacon update
        
    
        The browser extension reloads itself afterwards. Nothing to do in the browser.
    • 
      
  • Uninstall everything: ytm-beacon uninstall
        
    
        It stops the app and removes the start-at-login entry and the program. Add
          --purge to also delete your settings. Afterwards, remove the extension on your browser's
          extensions page (find YTM Beacon, click Remove).
